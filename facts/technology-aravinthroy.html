3 fun facts about Technology

By @aravinthroy

  1. All the electrons that make up the internet's active data weigh about 50 grams—roughly the weight of a single strawberry
  2. Apple's first logo featured Sir Isaac Newton sitting under an apple tree before it was replaced by the iconic bitten apple silhouette
  3. The Firefox logo is not a fox at all. The animal in the famous browser logo is actually a red panda.
